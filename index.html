Why Do Doctors Need Medical Indemnity Insurance?

Professional Indemnity Insurance helps doctors protect themselves against the financial consequences of eligible medico-legal claims arising from their professional services. It can provide support for legal defence costs and compensation liabilities, subject to policy terms and conditions. As medical practice evolves and patient expectations increase, having appropriate medical indemnity insurance forms an important part of professional risk management.

What Medical Indemnity Insurance Covers

  • Claims of medical negligence, error or omission
  • Legal defense and court fees
  • Compensation/damages awarded to a patient
  • Cover for past work (retroactive period)
  • Good Samaritan / emergency acts

What's Typically Not Covered

  • Deliberate or criminal acts
  • Treatment outside your registered specialty
  • Claims you knew about before the policy started
  • Cosmetic procedures (unless explicitly added)
  • Practising under the influence of intoxicants

Indicative only. Actual cover, limits and exclusions are as per the policy wording.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. How do I choose the right medical indemnity insurance?

Look beyond price. The right medical indemnity insurance is the plan whose indemnity limit, retroactive cover and exclusions match your specialty and claims risk — not simply the cheapest premium. Comparing plans side by side helps you see real value.

Q. Is medical indemnity insurance mandatory for doctors in India?

It is not legally mandatory for every doctor, but many hospitals, registration bodies and empanelment processes require proof of cover. Given rising litigation, most practising doctors treat it as essential rather than optional.

Q. What does medical indemnity insurance cost for doctors?

Medical indemnity insurance premiums depend on your specialty, years of experience, location and chosen indemnity limit. High-risk specialties pay more. The fastest way to know your price is to generate an instant quote.

Q. Does the policy cover claims from past treatments?

Many plans offer a retroactive period that can cover claims arising from work done before the policy start date, provided you were not already aware of the issue. Check the retroactive date on your chosen plan.

Q. How quickly am I covered?

In most cases, your medical indemnity insurance is issued digitally on the exact same day, immediately after your payment is processed securely.
